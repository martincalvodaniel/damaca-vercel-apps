# Product plan

Reusable Next.js starter with Google OAuth, MongoDB-backed access requests and grants, and administrator-managed access. No business product is defined.

## Accepted technical requirements

- Keep the stack and security boundaries in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

- Keep credentials local or in the deployment environment; `.env.example` contains placeholders only.
- Define product name, users, problem, MVP, and success criteria in iteration `01` before product implementation.
