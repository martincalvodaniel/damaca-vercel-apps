# Ebrias Patanes del Jurado

Club social y sus quedadas.

## Language

**Costume choice**:
The character a participant currently holds for an event.

**Costume attempt**:
A request to reserve a character other than the participant's current choice, whether the requested character is available or taken.

**Access request**:
A verified Google email waiting for an administrator to grant access to the club.

**Member**:
A person in the club roster who can attend multiple events and may have a linked Google email.

**Display alias**:
The name shown for a person in the club and its event rosters.

**Attendance**:
A member's participation status for a specific event.
