# Product plan

## Current state

Reusable, installable, offline-ready web app starter. Product data stays in IndexedDB on the current browser profile and device. No business product is defined.

## Accepted technical requirements

- Keep the static export, PWA, local storage, and security boundaries in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
- Do not imply backup, encryption, authentication, or cross-device sync.
- Define product name, users, problem, MVP, and success criteria in iteration `01` before product implementation.
- Agree on export and recovery before introducing irreplaceable product data.

## Open questions

No product domain, roadmap, backup policy, or deployment has been authorized.
